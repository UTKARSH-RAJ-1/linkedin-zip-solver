# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

Zip Puzzle Solution Overlay does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- The extension reads the puzzle board shown on the page (cell numbers and walls) only to compute a solution locally in your browser.
- It makes no network requests.
- It uses no cookies, analytics, tracking or remote code.
- It requests no browser permissions beyond running on the website pages listed in `manifest.json`.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
