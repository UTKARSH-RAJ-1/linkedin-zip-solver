# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

LinkedIn Games Solution Overlay (Unofficial) does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- The extension reads the puzzle board on the page (numbers, colours, symbols, digits) only to compute a solution locally in your browser.
- It makes no network requests and contains no analytics, tracking or remote code.
- It uses no cookies and no browser storage, and requests no browser permissions.
- The **Debug** button copies diagnostic text (page address and a small sample of the board's HTML) to **your clipboard** when you press it. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to paste it into a bug report.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
